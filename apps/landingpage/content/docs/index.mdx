## What is excited.live?

excited.live is a **free financial simulation tool**. You describe your life and money —
income, expenses, tax, goals — and it simulates years of your future in seconds, so you can
make big decisions with confidence.

## When does it launch?

We are preparing the first release now. Join the
[waitlist](/) to get early access, a founding-member reward, and priority support.

## Where do the numbers come from?

The simulation engine is open source — see the
[GitHub repository](https://github.com/fromsukong/excited.live). Thai tax rules are encoded
in a dedicated, tested engine.
