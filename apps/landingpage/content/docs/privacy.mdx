## Waitlist emails

When you join the waitlist, we store your **email address** and the language you were
browsing in. We use it only to:

- contact you when excited.live launches,
- deliver your early-access invite and founding-member reward.

We do not sell, rent, or share your email with third parties.

## Analytics and cookies

The marketing site sets **one preference cookie** (`exl-lang`) to remember whether you
prefer English or Thai. It contains no personal data. We do not run advertising trackers.

## Financial data you enter later

The simulation tool is designed so your plan data stays **in your browser** by default.
Nothing is uploaded until a feature explicitly asks for it.
